Community Guidelines

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Narrows is a community for boaters and anglers in Simcoe County and beyond. To keep it safe and welcoming, everyone who uses Narrows agrees to these Community Guidelines and to our Terms of Service. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

What is not allowed

Do not post, share, or send content that:

  • Harasses, bullies, or threatens another person, or incites others to do so.
  • Contains hate speech or attacks people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or any protected characteristic.
  • Is sexually explicit, pornographic, or nudity, or sexualizes minors in any way.
  • Depicts or promotes violence, self-harm, or threats of harm.
  • Is illegal, promotes illegal activity, or violates fishing/boating laws and regulations.
  • Is spam, a scam, or deliberately misleading.
  • Impersonates another person, business, or marina.
  • Infringes someone else's copyright, trademark, or other rights (only post photos and content you own or have permission to share).
  • Shares another person's private information without consent.

Report and block

You can report any post or comment from the "•••" menu (or by long-pressing a comment), and you can block any user so you no longer see their content and they no longer see yours. We encourage you to use these tools whenever something violates these guidelines.

What happens when content is reported

We review reports and act on objectionable content within 24 hours. Depending on severity, we may remove the content, issue a warning, suspend the account, or permanently remove the user and ban them from Narrows. Egregious or repeated violations result in immediate removal.

Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old to use Narrows. We do not knowingly allow accounts for anyone under 13.

Report a problem: Use the in-app report tools, or email support@narrows.site. We respond to all reports of objectionable content or abuse.
