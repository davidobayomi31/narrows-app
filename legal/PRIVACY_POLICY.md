## 1. Who we are

Canada]**. For any privacy question or request, contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## 2. Information we collect

**a. Account information**
- Email address and password (password is stored hashed by our auth provider).
- First and last name.

**b. Profile / onboarding information**
- Region, boat ownership, and experience level you provide during onboarding.
- Optional profile photo.

**c. Content you create**
- Posts (trips, catches, check-ins), captions, photos you upload.
- Likes and comments.
- Catch pins, fishing spots, and trip records, **including location coordinates**.

**d. Location information**
- With your permission, we collect your device's **GPS location** to show your
  position on the map, let you drop catch pins, and record trips.
- You can disable location access at any time in your device settings (some
  features will not work without it).

**e. Usage and device information**
- Basic technical data needed to run the App (e.g., app version, device type,
  error logs).

We do **not** intentionally collect financial information or government IDs.

## 3. How we use your information
- To provide and operate the App (accounts, feed, map, weather, trips).
- To display your name and content to other users (see "What's public" below).
- To improve features and fix problems.
- To communicate with you about your account (e.g., email confirmation, password
  reset).
- **[IF APPLICABLE]** To select and display advertising based on the onboarding
  information you provide (e.g., region, boat ownership, experience). See Section 6.

## 4. What is public vs. private
- **Public to other signed-in users:** your first/last name, profile photo,
  posts, captions, photos, likes, comments, and (where applicable) catch/trip
  locations you choose to share.
- **Private:** your email address and password are never shown to other users.
- Do not post anything you are not comfortable making visible to the community.

## 5. How we share information — service providers
We use trusted third parties to run the App. They process data only to provide
their service to us:
- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage (hosts your account
  and content).
- **Resend** — sending account emails (e.g., confirmation and password-reset codes).
- **Apple Maps / [MAP PROVIDER]** — displaying maps.
- **Open-Meteo** — weather data (we send only marina coordinates, not your
  personal information).
- **[RainViewer / others as added]** — weather radar imagery.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 6. Advertising **[INCLUDE IF/WHEN ADS ARE LIVE]**
We may show advertising in the App. Where we use your onboarding information
(such as region or boat ownership) to make ads more relevant, we will disclose
this and, where required by law, offer a way to opt out. We do not share your
identity (name/email) with advertisers.

## 7. Location data — specifics
- Location is used for: showing your position, catch pins, suggested spots, and
  trip tracking.
- Location is collected only while you use the relevant features (foreground).
- Catch/spot/trip locations you save are stored in our database; you control
  whether you create them, and you can delete them.

## 8. Data retention
We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete
your account, your profile and associated content are deleted (some data may
remain in backups for a limited period, and we may retain limited records where
required by law).
